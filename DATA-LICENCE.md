# Data licence and attribution

Goldilocks Map contains climate metrics derived from Met Office HadUK-Grid gridded climate observations.

## Licence

HadUK-Grid is © Crown copyright and is provided under the [Open Government Licence v3.0](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/).

Suggested attribution for Goldilocks Map and its derived climate rasters:

> Contains Met Office HadUK-Grid data © Crown copyright, licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. Goldilocks Map climate metrics are derived from HadUK-Grid and are not an official Met Office product.

## Historical dataset citation

The stable 2016–2025 observations used by the current Goldilocks Map build come from the citable CEDA HadUK-Grid v1.3.2.ceda release:

Met Office; Hollis, D.; Carlisle, E.; Kendon, M.; Packman, S.; Doherty, A. (2026): *HadUK-Grid Gridded Climate Observations on a 1km grid over the UK, v1.3.2.ceda (1836-2025).* NERC EDS Centre for Environmental Data Analysis, 23 June 2026. [doi:10.5285/789b3065d74a4c948ab05d33556c86d0](https://doi.org/10.5285/789b3065d74a4c948ab05d33556c86d0).

## Provisional 2026 data

The current build also includes published 2026 provisional HadUK-Grid temperature grids from the [Met Office HadUK-Grid provisional-data service](https://www.metoffice.gov.uk/hadobs/hadukgrid/). These provisional grids are also made available under the Open Government Licence, but the Met Office states that they may be amended or revised before becoming part of a later citable annual CEDA release.

## Method reference

Hollis, D.; McCarthy, M. P.; Kendon, M.; Legg, T.; Simpson, I. (2019): *HadUK-Grid — A new UK dataset of gridded climate observations.* Geoscience Data Journal, 6, 151–159. [doi:10.1002/gdj3.78](https://doi.org/10.1002/gdj3.78).

## What Goldilocks publishes

The authenticated CEDA source NetCDF archive is build input and is not committed or published by this project. The public snapshot contains only Goldilocks-derived, quantised climate metric rasters plus the metadata needed to interpret them. The derivation, threshold choices, quality-control masking, quantisation, LOD construction and presentation are Goldilocks Map processing choices rather than an official Met Office product.
